Teaching Theory

Specialized workshops and weekly studio classes.

As an experienced art instructor with more than 12 years of teaching experience, I enjoy helping students of all ages discover and develop their artistic potential. My background includes a Master's degree in Landscape Architecture and extensive experience in drawing, painting, acrylics, oils, colored pencils, and mixed media.


My teaching philosophy encourages creativity, confidence, and self-expression while building a strong foundation in artistic techniques. Every student is encouraged to explore their unique artistic voice in a supportive and inspiring environment.
